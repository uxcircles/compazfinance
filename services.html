Bridging loans

Buying a new property before selling the one you have.

Bridging loans are short-term loans secured on property, usually repaid within 12 months, sometimes within weeks. They come as open loans (no fixed end date, usually repaid within 12 to 24 months) or closed loans (a fixed repayment date, usually cheaper), and as first or second charge, depending on whether there is already a mortgage on the property.

They can make a quick purchase possible, but they cost more than a normal mortgage and the property is at risk if you can't repay. You need a clear plan for how the loan will be paid off, and we help you test that plan before you commit.

  • Market research
  • Expert advice
  • Negotiation
  • Streamlined process
  • Customised solution
  • Legal and regulatory compliance

The Financial Conduct Authority does not regulate most forms of bridging loans.

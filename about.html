Set up by Lily Liu in 2020

Compaz Finance was established in 2020 by Lily Liu, drawing on her extensive experience in the UK mortgage industry and her fluency in English and Mandarin.

Since then the firm has served hundreds of clients across the UK, including homeowners, landlords and property investors, with friendly, sound advice and practical help.

Clients know the firm for diligence, care and, above all, trustworthiness. Each customer is treated as an individual: we listen first and recommend second.
